01
Bounded execution
At most eight model attempts per review, including retries. One retry for transient errors, a 30-second per-call limit and a 120-second deadline. Local timeout does not guarantee provider cancellation.
02
Traceable sources
Contract evidence must match its input span. Knowledge citations must match sources retrieved for that run. Three Chinese Civil Code summaries provide limited context; English glosses are unreviewed translations.
03
Visible failure
Stage errors, partial results and insufficient evidence remain explicit. The testing API returns results directly without storing review history. Generated revisions require human review.




03 / WHAT THE TRIAL TAUGHT US
Completion is not correctness.
Both fictional contracts completed the recorded trial. Reviewing the actual output still exposed errors.
OBSERVED FAILURE
A cap labeled “unlimited”
The complete example capped liability at the purchase price. A model finding nevertheless used the unlimited_liability category.
Subsequent offline check: recognized contradictions stop the risk stage instead of silently relabeling them.
OBSERVED FAILURE
A known quantity lost
A proposed revision replaced “one unit” with a quantity placeholder. Preserving original evidence did not prevent the draft from losing a fact.
Subsequent offline check: loss of recognized values rejects the revision and produces a partial result.
Deliberately limited scope
These lexical checks can miss semantic errors and reject valid rewording. They do not validate legal reasoning. The latest policy has not been rerun live. Text and TXT only; no Canadian-law processing, public review service, or legal-accuracy claim.
